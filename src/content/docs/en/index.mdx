RSLive is a practical encyclopedia about relocation, documents, institutions and everyday life in Serbia. Most of the encyclopedia is written in Russian; the English edition is a **selective edition**, not a machine-translated copy of the whole site.

It focuses on material that is useful on its own in English: information about RSLive, procedures that can be shared with an English-speaking landlord or other counterpart, and reference articles explaining Russian migration and Russian organisations in Serbia.

## About RSLive

- **[About RSLive](/en/about/)** — how the encyclopedia is edited, checked and corrected.
- **[RSLive stickers](/en/about/stickers/)** — the sticker pack, Open Graph illustrations and how they are used on the site.
- **[Pig Peter and the RSLive mascot](/en/pig-peter/)** — the origin of the mascot and its Russian internet and emigration context.

## Guides

- **[How to register a foreigner's stay through eUprava](/en/foreigner-residence-registration/)** — a standalone guide for a property owner: eID, ConsentID, a qualified electronic certificate and the electronic registration procedure.

## Russians in Serbia

- **[Russians in Serbia](/en/russians-in-serbia/)** — an overview of the different communities and institutions and why they should not be treated as one organisation.
- **[Demographics](/en/russians-in-serbia/demographics/)** — temporary residence, permanent residence, citizenship data and how to read the statistics correctly.
- **[KSORS](/en/russians-in-serbia/ksors/)** — the Coordinating Council of Organisations of Russian Compatriots in Serbia and its member organisations.
- **[National Council of the Russian National Minority](/en/russians-in-serbia/national-council/)** — its legal status in Serbia, powers, elections and activities.
- **[Russian House in Belgrade](/en/russians-in-serbia/russian-house/)** — its history, institutional status and programmes.

The English edition grows when a topic benefits from a self-contained English-language page. It does not attempt to mirror every Russian-language article.
