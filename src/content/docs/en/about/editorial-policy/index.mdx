## Hierarchy of sources

When preparing practical guides, we use sources in the following order:

1. Laws and secondary legislation of the Republic of Serbia.
2. Official websites of government authorities and electronic services.
3. Written explanations from institutions and professional organizations.
4. Publications by specialist lawyers, accountants, doctors and other professionals.
5. Verifiable practical experience reported by readers.
6. Reports from local communities, only as leads for further verification.

Whenever possible, a statement about a legal rule is accompanied by a link to the specific document or official source. The practice of an individual institution is not presented as a nationwide rule.

A specialist's comment supplements the primary source but does not replace it. References to professional analyses identify the author, their field of expertise, publication or update date, and the specific material. The editorial team distinguishes between quoting a specialist, obtaining a comment for an article and having the entire article reviewed.

## Keeping information up to date

A page may display two different dates:

- **Last updated** is derived from Git history and indicates the last text edit. Correcting a typo or an internal link also changes this date.
- **Sources checked** is entered manually after the editorial team verifies substantive claims against current primary sources. It does not automatically change whenever a page is edited.

Neither date guarantees that a government authority, bank, insurer, clinic or other external source has not changed its rules since the check. Before taking action with legal, financial or medical consequences, consult the cited primary source directly.

Do not use a dynamic “updated today” label unless the material was checked that day. Such a label creates a false impression of currency and cannot replace substantive source verification.

Information about migration, taxes, government fees, banking and medicine requires especially frequent checks. If the source verification date is old or missing, consult the cited primary source before making a decision.

## Authorship and review

An individual author is named only when their contribution can be linked to a specific publication. If no individual author is named, the RSLive editorial team is responsible for the publication.

A reviewer or specialist is named only with their consent. The absence of a reviewer does not mean the material constitutes professional advice.

A link to a specialist's published work does not imply that they reviewed the RSLive article, endorse its conclusions or are associated with the project. The status “reviewed by” is used only after direct agreement on the specific version of the material.

## Corrections

Factual errors are corrected in the main text. Significant changes to requirements, deadlines or procedures must be accompanied by updated sources and their verification date.

Report an error through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Antiokh/rslive_content/issues/new). Ideally, include:

- the page URL;
- the incorrect or outdated statement;
- a current primary source;
- the date on which the information was checked.

## Using our materials

When paraphrasing or quoting, we recommend linking to the specific page rather than only to the home page. This lets readers see the context, sources, last modification date and source verification date.
