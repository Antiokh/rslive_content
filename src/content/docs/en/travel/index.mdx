## National parks

The National Tourism Organisation of Serbia lists five national parks: Fruška Gora, Đerdap, Tara, Kopaonik and Šar-planina.[^1] Use current park and tourism organisation websites rather than old lists of areas, trails and access rules: boundaries and conditions may change.

### Fruška Gora

The most convenient national park for a short trip from Novi Sad; the tourism organisation suggests approximately half an hour from the city. Forest walks, monasteries and wine-growing areas can be combined here.[^2]

Choose a particular destination or trail in advance: the park is too large to cover in one day. The [separate itinerary](/lifestyle/travel/fruska-gora-sremski-karlovci/) (Russian) combines Sremski Karlovci with official GPX routes.

### Đerdap

The park stretches along the Danube from the Golubac area towards Kladovo and includes the Iron Gates gorge, natural areas and archaeological and historical sites. At the source check on 4 September 2026 the park gave an area of 63,786.48 hectares; do not treat larger figures from older publications as current.[^3]

Some walking routes have controlled access. The park states that most developed trails in its first-degree protection zone require prior arrangements; the exception in its list is the Zlatno jezero educational trail.[^4]

<Aside type="tip" title="Plan Đerdap in advance">
  If you want to visit a particular trail, viewpoint or protected zone, contact the park before travelling. Do not assume every trail marked online is open without restrictions.
</Aside>

### Tara

Tara suits a multi-day nature trip: forests, Zaovine, the Drina valley and nearby Šargan–Mokra Gora offer different routes in one region. One symbol of the park is the Serbian spruce, Pančićeva omorika.[^5]

For mountain trips, check the forecast and road conditions for your specific destination, especially outside summer.

### Kopaonik

Kopaonik is both a national park and a major mountain tourism area. In winter, operating ski facilities matter most; in warmer months, the specific walking route and protected-zone rules are more important.[^1]

Do not assume resort rules apply throughout the park. See the [winter travel guide](/lifestyle/travel/winter-serbia/) (Russian).

### Šar-planina

Serbia's official tourism organisation includes Šar-planina among its five national parks.[^1] Travelling there requires separate preparation concerning routes, border and administrative conditions, transport and access to the particular area. The national parks list is not sufficient as a travel instruction.

## How to use this section

Before travelling, check:

- opening hours and seasonality of each attraction;
- protected-area access rules and booking requirements;
- road conditions and the weather forecast;
- public transport timetables if travelling without a car;
- mobile coverage and availability of cashless payments in remote areas.

Do not rely solely on automatic navigation routes in mountainous or rural areas.
