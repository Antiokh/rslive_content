## Data and editorial review

This map combines the previously reviewed [Russian-speaking cafés, bars and shops](/en/russian-places-serbia/) with additional businesses from the editorial directory. New locations appear once their public coordinates have been reviewed. Inclusion is not an endorsement of a business or its services.
