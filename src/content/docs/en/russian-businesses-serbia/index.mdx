This **RSLive editorial map** brings together Russian-speaking and Russian-community cafés, restaurants, bars, shops, services, education, beauty and wellness, and leisure businesses in Serbia. Use the map's **category filter** to view one type of business at a time.

**Red markers** indicate a documented connection to ALTA banka, with the evidence source identified in the card. **Blue markers** mean that this connection has not yet been verified; they do not establish that a business had no ALTA account or suffered no loss. The presence of an account at another bank does not negate a confirmed ALTA connection. An ALTA account alone does not establish the amount of loss or the card-payment acquirer.[^1][^2]

## Data and editorial review

This map combines the previously reviewed [Russian-speaking cafés, bars and shops](/en/russian-places-serbia/) with additional businesses from the editorial directory. New locations appear once their public coordinates have been reviewed. Inclusion is not an endorsement of a business or its services.
