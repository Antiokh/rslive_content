Do not plan a winter trip in Serbia by the month alone. Snow, snowmaking, wind and technical readiness determine which slopes and lifts operate **on a particular day**. Before booking, consult the official `Ski info`, slope status and webcams.

The National Tourism Organisation of Serbia identifies Kopaonik, Tornik at Zlatibor and Stara Planina as the main developed ski centres. In snowy conditions, smaller winter centres are also available at Divčibare, Tara, Zlatar and Goč. Check whether the lifts actually operate before travelling.[^1]

## In brief

- **Kopaonik** — the widest choice of slopes and lifts: over 64 km of slopes and ski routes, night skiing and three beginner areas.[^2]
- **Tornik** — a separate ski centre about 9 km from Zlatibor's tourist centre, at elevations of 1,110–1,490 m.[^3]
- **Divčibare** — a smaller resort on Mount Maljen, suitable for a short trip, with several slopes of varying difficulty.[^4]
- **Stara Planina** — another major `Skijališta Srbije` centre; check its dedicated `Ski info` immediately before departure.[^1]

<Aside type="caution" title="Artificial snow does not guarantee an open slope">
  Snowmaking makes the season more reliable but does not eliminate the effects of temperature, wind, maintenance or the operator's decisions. Even at Kopaonik, where most of the ski area has snowmaking coverage, the status of each slope is published separately.[^2][^5]
</Aside>

## Kopaonik

`Skijališta Srbije` lists more than **64 km** of prepared slopes and ski routes at Kopaonik, three illuminated slopes for night skiing and three `ski vrtić` areas for beginners. Snowmaking covers 97% of the area.[^2]

This is the best option for a wide choice of difficulty levels and several days of skiing. However, the size of the resort does not mean all its slopes are open at the same time.

Before buying a ski pass:

1. open the `Staze i ski putevi` page;
2. check the `Prohodno` column for your chosen slopes;
3. check the time of the last update;
4. consult the webcams and weather forecast;
5. only then choose a particular part of the resort, parking area and lift.[^5]

The 2025/26 season illustrated why calendar-based assumptions are unreliable: following fresh snowfall, the operator extended expanded operations at Kopaonik until mid-April 2026.[^2]

## Zlatibor and Tornik

Tornik is **9 km** from Zlatibor's tourist centre and operates as a separate ski centre. The operator lists elevations of 1,110–1,490 m, a six-seat chairlift, two drag lifts and five slopes with snowmaking.[^3]

Do not book accommodation in central Zlatibor expecting to walk straight out of the hotel onto a slope. Arrange transport to Tornik in advance.

In the 2025/26 season the operator officially ended winter skiing at Tornik on **8 March 2026**.[^3] This is an example from a specific season, not an annual closing date.

## Divčibare

Divčibare is located on Mount Maljen at an elevation of about 980 m. The National Tourism Organisation describes the approximately 600-metre `Centar` slope and several smaller slopes for beginners; night skiing is possible on `Centar`.[^4]

Choose Divčibare for a short winter getaway when you do not need infrastructure on Kopaonik's scale. Before leaving, check actual lift operation and snow conditions with the local operator.

## Stara Planina

Stara Planina is part of the `Skijališta Srbije` network alongside Kopaonik and Tornik.[^1] The same principle applies: do not carry over last season's schedule to the current winter. Check the official page for slope and lift operation.

If skiing is your main objective, choose accommodation according to access to the ski centre rather than distance from the geographical label `Stara planina`.

## If you do not ski

A winter trip can include walking, sledding, wellness and city activities. But driving into the mountains in winter requires the same preparation:

- check road conditions and the weather forecast;
- allow for early darkness;
- do not follow a summer hiking route covered with snow and ice if you lack winter hiking preparation;
- check national park access rules separately for natural areas.

Spa towns and thermal options are covered in [Serbian spa resorts](/en/serbian-spa-resorts/).

## Checks before departure

<Steps>
1. A few days before travelling, choose one or two centres according to their infrastructure.
2. On the day of booking, check the official `Ski info`, not a search result snippet.
3. The day before leaving, consult slope status, webcams, wind and precipitation forecasts.
4. On the morning of the trip, check again: the operator may close an individual lift because of weather or technical problems.
5. Do not buy equipment or a ski pass for a slope that is not listed as open.
</Steps>
