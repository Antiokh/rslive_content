**Ark Business** (Kovcheg Business, «Ковчег Бизнес») has published an external map of businesses that its organisers describe as affected by the withdrawal of ALTA banka's banking licence.[^1] RSLive presents this as a **third-party source** and has not independently checked each company's accounts, financial losses or payment arrangements.

<Aside type="caution" title="Status of the organisers under Russian law">
  **Russia's Ministry of Justice included the “Kovcheg” (“Ark”) project in its register of “foreign agents” on 1 December 2023, entry No. 727.**[^2] Ark Business describes itself as an initiative established within the Kovcheg project.[^3] The map below is therefore produced by an initiative associated with a project designated as a foreign agent **under Russian law**.

  This status **does not mean that the businesses shown on the map are themselves designated as foreign agents**, nor that they belong to the organisation. RSLive does not operate the map, vet applications or partner with the organisers.
</Aside>

## Submitting a business to the external map

**The “Zajedno uz ruske biznise” spreadsheet is not operated by Ark Business.** In a public reply, an account whose author identified themselves as the Ark Business project lead explained that it is a separate initiative organised by Serbian participants. Ark Business uses businesses already verified through that spreadsheet, but does not administer it.[^5]

To submit a business **to the Serbian initiative**, use its [public “NOVI UNOSI - PENDING VERIFIKACIJA” tab](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IBRo4Frwh6ph5q1Bykk8JeNIzJwntW5JVyS1YGQXJCE/edit?gid=418527537#gid=418527537). **The spreadsheet's own instructions** request a business name, activity, city, public contact details and a **Serbian company registration number (MB) or tax identification number (PIB)**. Its moderators check whether ALTA banka appears among the company's accounts and reject entries that do not meet those verification requirements.[^4] The [“Verifikovani biznisi” tab](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IBRo4Frwh6ph5q1Bykk8JeNIzJwntW5JVyS1YGQXJCE/edit?gid=0#gid=0) contains records verified **by the spreadsheet initiative**, not necessarily by Ark Business.

Separately, Ark Business receives requests through the project's direct messages and, according to its representative, **independently verifies** those submissions.[^5] You can find the project's public channels on the [official Ark Business website](https://kovcheg.business/). Adding an entry to the Serbian spreadsheet is not a direct application to Ark Business and does not guarantee inclusion on its map. The spreadsheet is public; do not disclose confidential banking information or private personal details.

**The maps have different scopes.** According to the project representative, the present map concerns only businesses affected by the withdrawal of ALTA banka's licence. Ark Business is also developing a separate worldwide map of businesses founded by Russian entrepreneurs; that future product is distinct from this ALTA-specific map.[^5]
