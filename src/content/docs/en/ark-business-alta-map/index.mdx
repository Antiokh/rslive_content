**Ark Business** (Kovcheg Business, «Ковчег Бизнес») has published an external map of businesses that its organisers describe as affected by the withdrawal of ALTA banka's banking licence.[^1] RSLive presents this as a **third-party source** and has not independently checked each company's accounts, financial losses or payment arrangements.

<Aside type="caution" title="Status of the organisers under Russian law">
  **Russia's Ministry of Justice included the “Kovcheg” (“Ark”) project in its register of “foreign agents” on 1 December 2023, entry No. 727.**[^2] Ark Business describes itself as an initiative established within the Kovcheg project.[^3] The map below is therefore produced by an initiative associated with a project designated as a foreign agent **under Russian law**.

  This status **does not mean that the businesses shown on the map are themselves designated as foreign agents**, nor that they belong to the organisation. RSLive does not operate the map, vet applications or partner with the organisers.
</Aside>

## Submitting a business to the external map

Ark Business uses a [public spreadsheet for new submissions (“NOVI UNOSI - PENDING VERIFIKACIJA”)](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IBRo4Frwh6ph5q1Bykk8JeNIzJwntW5JVyS1YGQXJCE/edit?gid=418527537#gid=418527537). The organisers ask for a business name, type, city, public contact details and its **Serbian company registration number (MB) or tax identification number (PIB)**. Their stated verification process checks whether ALTA banka appears among the company's bank accounts. Submissions without the identifying information or the relevant bank account are removed.[^4]

The [“Verifikovani biznisi” tab](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IBRo4Frwh6ph5q1Bykk8JeNIzJwntW5JVyS1YGQXJCE/edit?gid=0#gid=0) contains businesses verified **by the organisers**, not independently by RSLive. The spreadsheet is public, and submitting information means sharing it with a separate project. Do not publish private banking credentials or other confidential information.

## RSLive's independent directory

The [RSLive map of Russian-speaking businesses](/en/russian-businesses-serbia/) covers reviewed cafés, shops and other categories, **without classifying them by their relationship to ALTA banka**. Businesses may also be submitted to the separate [RSLive editorial directory](/en/russian-business-directory/). External map records are not imported automatically.
