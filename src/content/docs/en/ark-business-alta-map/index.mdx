**Ark Business** (Kovcheg Business, «Ковчег Бизнес») has published an external map of businesses that its organisers describe as affected by the withdrawal of ALTA banka's banking licence.[^1] RSLive presents this as a **third-party source** and has not independently checked each company's accounts, financial losses or payment arrangements.

<Aside type="caution" title="Status of the organisers under Russian law">
  **Russia's Ministry of Justice included the “Kovcheg” (“Ark”) project in its register of “foreign agents” on 1 December 2023, entry No. 727.**[^2] Ark Business describes itself as an initiative established within the Kovcheg project.[^3] The map below is therefore produced by an initiative associated with a project designated as a foreign agent **under Russian law**.

  This status **does not mean that the businesses shown on the map are themselves designated as foreign agents**, nor that they belong to the organisation. RSLive does not operate the map, vet applications or partner with the organisers.
</Aside>

## Submitting a business to the external map

**To suggest a business directly to Ark Business, message the team on Telegram: [@kovchegbusiness](https://t.me/kovchegbusiness).** The project's announcement explicitly provides this contact. A person identifying themselves as the project lead said the team independently checks submissions received in direct messages.[^5][^6]

**The “Zajedno uz ruske biznise” spreadsheet is a separate Serbian initiative.** Ark Business credits Threads user **Sanja Simić** with starting it.[^6] The sheet contains a [new-submissions tab](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IBRo4Frwh6ph5q1Bykk8JeNIzJwntW5JVyS1YGQXJCE/edit?gid=418527537#gid=418527537) and a [verified-records tab](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1IBRo4Frwh6ph5q1Bykk8JeNIzJwntW5JVyS1YGQXJCE/edit?gid=0#gid=0). According to the spreadsheet's own instructions, administrators require a **Serbian company registration number (MB) or tax identification number (PIB)** and check whether the company had an account at ALTA banka.[^4] Ark Business uses vetted entries from this independent spreadsheet but operates its own map and verification channel.[^5] Submitting to the sheet is not the same as contacting Ark Business.

**Scope and size.** The organisers say this map covers businesses affected by the withdrawal of ALTA banka's licence. Their announcement reports **more than 100 venues**; RSLive has not independently counted or verified them.[^6] Ark Business has also announced a separate, forthcoming worldwide map of businesses founded by Russian entrepreneurs.[^5]

The spreadsheet is public. Do not submit personal banking credentials or confidential information. Neither submission route guarantees inclusion.
