This directory covers Russian-speaking and Russian-community **cafés, restaurants, bars, shops, services, beauty and wellness, education, and leisure businesses** in Serbia. Search by name, category or city, then follow the organisation's public contact link. Inclusion does not mean RSLive recommends or guarantees the quality of its products or services.

<Aside type="note" title="Supporting local businesses">
  Following events involving ALTA banka, members of the community have encouraged customers to support local Russian-speaking entrepreneurs. You can do so by shopping, booking services or visiting a venue. **Being listed here does not mean a business used ALTA banka or suffered financial losses.** For businesses dealing with funds at that bank, see the [Russian-language guide to ALTA banka refunds](/arrival/bank/alta/refund/).
</Aside>

## Separate external initiative after ALTA banka

The [Ark Business map](/en/ark-business-alta-map/) is a third-party list of businesses whose organisers say they were affected by the banking situation. Its page explains the organisers' status under Russian law and their application and verification process. It is not part of RSLive's editorial directory.
