# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data **RSLive (rslive.ru)** may process, why it is used, and how you can request access, correction, or deletion.

## Data we may process

RSLive may process:

- information that you voluntarily send to the editorial team when contacting us, submitting material, or reporting an error;
- technical data required to deliver the website, diagnose errors, and protect infrastructure, such as IP address, user agent, request time, requested URL, and technical request status;
- data received through external APIs and integrations, including Meta Platform, only within the permissions granted by the relevant platform and for the functionality for which the data was obtained.

No account is required to read the public encyclopedia content.

## How data is used

Data is used only to the extent necessary to:

- operate, secure, and support the website;
- respond to user requests;
- verify, update, and publish reference information;
- operate connected integrations;
- comply with applicable legal obligations where required.

RSLive does not sell users' personal data.

## Meta Platform data

If RSLive receives data through Meta products or APIs, that data is used only for the stated function of the relevant integration and within the permissions granted by Meta.

Such data is not used to sell personal information or to create independent advertising profiles. Access is limited to what is necessary for the integration. Data is deleted or no longer used when it is no longer needed for the stated purpose, when access is revoked, or when deletion is required by applicable rules or law.

## Sharing with third parties

Data may be processed by technical providers that support hosting, security, website delivery, or external integrations. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or by a binding request from a competent authority.

## Retention

Data is retained no longer than necessary for its processing purpose, technical security, dispute resolution, or mandatory legal requirements. Retention periods for technical logs may depend on the configuration of the infrastructure and service providers used by RSLive.

## Access, correction, and deletion

You may request information about data relating to you, as well as its correction or deletion where there is no mandatory reason to keep it.

To make a request, contact the RSLive editorial team via [Telegram](https://t.me/rslive_ru). Do not publish sensitive personal data in public messages or public issues.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, integrations, or data-processing requirements change. The current version is always published on this page.
