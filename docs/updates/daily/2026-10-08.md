# Обновления за 08.10.2026

- [From self-employment to employment in Serbia](/en/employing-foreigners-serbia/change-work-basis/) — Added an English guide to changing a single-permit work basis from self-employment to local employment, including NSZ consent, PPZ, CROSO, B2B transition risks and work already performed.
